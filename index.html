我們的理念Our Philosophy

把星野，變成教室。The world is our classroom.

自然是最好的教室。我們是一群來自不同科學領域的大學生，帶著望遠鏡與好奇心，和各地的孩子一起認識星空、觀察環境，讓課本裡的科學成為親手探索的經驗。Nature is our best classroom. As university students from different fields of science, we bring telescopes and curiosity to communities, exploring the sky and the environment with children through hands-on learning.

從家鄉的土地與故事出發，我們設計貼近在地的天文與 STEM 課程，希望讓孩子保有提問的勇氣，也更認識、珍惜自己生活的地方。Local landscapes and stories shape our astronomy and STEM activities. We hope children keep asking questions, feel connected to where they live, and learn to care for it.

我們期待透過持續合作，讓更多孩子接觸不同的學習機會，把對自然的好奇，慢慢變成探索與守護環境的行動。Through lasting partnerships, we hope to open up more learning opportunities and help curiosity grow into exploration and care for the natural world.
