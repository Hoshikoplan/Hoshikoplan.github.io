義盛國小天文與自然科學營 Yisheng Elementary School Astronomy Camp

📅 2024年06月28日 - 06月30日 June 28 - June 30, 2024
📍 桃園市復興區義盛國小 Yisheng Elementary School, Fuxing District, Taoyuan
👥 13名小朋友 13 Children

我們在桃園市復興區義盛國小舉辦了為期三天的天文與自然科學營隊，這是「星野的孩子」團隊首次舉辦的活動。 We held a three-day astronomy and nature science camp at Yisheng Elementary School in Fuxing District, Taoyuan City. This was the first event hosted by the "Hoshiko" team.

我們帶領小朋友認識基本的天文知識，包括星座辨識、太陽故事、實際觀測等。通過望遠鏡觀測和互動式的天文學習，孩子們對天文產生了濃厚的興趣。 We guided the children to learn basic astronomical knowledge, including constellation identification, stories of the sun, and actual observation. Through telescope observation and interactive astronomy learning, the children developed a strong interest in astronomy.

我們設計了多個生態與環境的課程與實驗，讓孩子們動手操作，了解火山的成因。並透過實地踏查學校生態，讓小朋友能更深入地了解生活環境。 We designed various ecology and environment courses and experiments, allowing children to learn about the causes of volcanoes through hands-on activities. We also conducted field trips to explore the school's ecology, helping children gain a deeper understanding of their living environment.

觀測當晚一開始烏雲密布，還有雷雨，我們便先把課程移到室內。約晚上 11 點雲層散去，團隊立刻將器材搬到戶外，和孩子們一起觀星。 Clouds and thunderstorms greeted us at the start of the observing night, so we moved the lesson indoors. Around 11 p.m., the clouds cleared. We carried the equipment outside and observed the night sky with the children.

這次活動不僅讓孩子們學習到了天文知識，更重要的是激發了他們對自然科學的熱情與好奇心。我們也從這次經驗中學習，為未來的活動做更好的準備。 This event not only taught the children astronomical knowledge but, most importantly, sparked their passion and curiosity for natural science. We also learned from this experience to better prepare for future activities.

本次活動感謝臺大教學發展中心「小田野計畫」資助，「鴻宇光學」提供儀器與贈送。 We thank the NTU Center for Teaching and Learning Development "Little Field Project" for funding, and "Galux Optical" for providing and donating instruments.
